# Content notes
JEELE is an independent, editorial exploration guide. It is not a booking operator, accredited training organisation, job board, qualification assessor or financial advisor. The website is based on the accompanying field guide, with 30 sport pathways and six adjacent careers.

Research checked: 6 October 2026.
- Source links in `assets/js/data.js` support specific published cost/credential claims.
- Regions are examples to investigate, not independently confirmed live schools or vacancies.
- Relative spending labels are not professional startup quotations.
- The starter-plan builder is rule-based, not an AI assessment. It does not evaluate fitness, legal eligibility, available money or training experience.
- It does not promise earnings or cost recovery.
- No comparable verified salary dataset was established; the site intentionally does not invent pay or best-ROI rankings.
- Tourist package fees must not be interpreted as worker take-home.
- Sporting certifications, operator permissions and legal work rights are separate.
- The bundled PDF is anonymised for public hosting; no private user name/email is included.
- External source links are not endorsements, affiliations or permission to use organisations' trademarks.

Extreme sports can cause serious injury or death. Use qualified supervision, suitable equipment, current medical guidance and activity-specific insurance. Check local/state rules, permits, professional scope, emergency plans, and currency before participating or selling instruction.

Update prices and requirements from the linked provider/bodies before portraying them as current. Publish only content and photos you have permission to share.
